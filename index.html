ShortPaste Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

ShortPaste is a custom keyboard app that lets users create and manage text shortcuts and saved images for quick access from the iOS keyboard.

1. Information stored by ShortPaste

ShortPaste stores the text shortcuts, folder information, and images that you choose to add to the app. This information is stored locally on your device using Apple's app storage and shared app storage required for the ShortPaste keyboard extension.

ShortPaste does not upload these user-created texts or images to ShortPaste servers.

2. Photos and Files

ShortPaste may access photos or files only when you explicitly choose to import an image using the corresponding iOS interface. Imported images are stored locally for use by ShortPaste and its keyboard extension.

3. Clipboard

When you choose to copy an image from the ShortPaste keyboard, ShortPaste places the selected image on the iOS system clipboard so that it can be pasted into another app. ShortPaste does not send the clipboard contents to its own servers.

The availability and handling of clipboard contents are also subject to Apple's iOS system behavior and the settings of other apps and services you use.

4. Keyboard Extension and Full Access

ShortPaste includes a custom keyboard extension. The extension uses Apple's shared app storage to access the shortcuts and images you have saved in ShortPaste.

The keyboard extension may require iOS's “Allow Full Access” setting for the functionality provided by the keyboard. ShortPaste does not use this access to collect or transmit your keystrokes or personal content to ShortPaste servers.

5. Data collection, analytics, advertising, and tracking

ShortPaste does not operate its own analytics, advertising, tracking, or user-profiling systems. ShortPaste does not sell or share your saved shortcuts or images with third parties.

6. Accounts

ShortPaste does not require you to create a ShortPaste account.

7. Data deletion

You can delete shortcuts, folders, and saved images using the controls provided in ShortPaste. Because user content is stored locally, deleting the app may also remove locally stored ShortPaste data according to Apple's iOS behavior.

8. Children's privacy

ShortPaste does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
